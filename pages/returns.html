Right of withdrawal

Under EU consumer law, retail customers may withdraw from a purchase within 14 days of delivery, provided products are unopened and in original condition. Some sealed cosmetic and hygiene products are exempt once opened.

How to return

  1. Contact us via the contact page with your order number.
  2. We’ll confirm the return address and instructions.
  3. Ship the items back in their original packaging.

Refunds

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method via Stripe, typically within 5–10 business days of receiving the return.

Trade orders

Wholesale (trade) orders are governed by your trade agreement. Please contact us to arrange any return.
